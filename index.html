📘 इस्तेमाल कैसे करें

  1. Upload: Apne bank ka PDF statement (savings/current account) select karein ya drag-drop karein.
  2. Analyze: "Analyze Statement" par click karein — tool PDF ko browser mein hi padhta hai, kahin bhejta nahi.
  3. Summary check karein: Total credit, debit, net balance aur category-wise kharcha (Salary, Shopping, Food, Bills, ATM, Transfer, Others) turant dikhega.
  4. Preview: Pehle 10 transactions free mein dikhte hain, taaki quality check kar sakein.
  5. Unlock: Pura statement aur Excel download ke liye ek baar ka lifetime unlock payment karein (Razorpay se, UPI/card sab chalega).
  6. Download: "Download Excel" dabate hi ek .xlsx file milegi jisme 2 sheets hongi — Transactions aur Summary. Client ko seedha bhej sakte hain.
  7. Tip: Scanned/image-based PDF statements (jinme text select nahi hota) is tool mein accurately nahi parse honge — sirf digital/text-based PDF statements par best result milta hai.
